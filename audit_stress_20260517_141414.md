## Claim #2 — Misclassified penalty/fine canary metrics

These metrics' names suggest lower-is-better, but the keyword logic in `core/scoring.py` classifies them as higher-is-better:

| Metric | Matched word | Classified as | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| `regulatory_fine_amount` | `fine` | higher-is-better | **lower-is-better** |
| `outstanding_lawsuits` | `lawsuit` | higher-is-better | **lower-is-better** |
| `system_downtime_pct` | `downtime` | higher-is-better | **lower-is-better** |
| `total_penalties_ytd` | `penalt` | higher-is-better | **lower-is-better** |

**Consequence:** when these metrics go UP (worse for the business), the system treats it as IMPROVEMENT — inflating the grade and the wrong direction for goals.
